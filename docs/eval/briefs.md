# Twenty house briefs, for the authoring eval

Written before either set of documents, and never changed afterwards. Each is a sentence
or two — the kind of thing a person actually says to an agent — and each is deliberately
small (two to six rooms) so that what is being measured is the *syntax*, not stamina.

Between them they reach every part of the schema that is not trivially shared: levels and
a stair, a void, a track grid, fixtures, outdoor spaces, a courtyard, `covered`, `glazed`,
`entrance`, an authored id, `at` placement, a `poly` room and a `depth`.

1. **Studio.** One room with a kitchenette, a shower room beside it, and a front door to the street.
2. **One-bedroom flat.** Living-and-kitchen, a bedroom, a bathroom, all off a small hall.
3. **Two-bedroom flat.** Two bedrooms and a bathroom off a corridor, living room at one end.
4. **Cottage with a porch.** Three rooms, and a covered porch across the south side.
5. **Garage workshop.** A double garage with a small office boxed into one corner.
6. **Bungalow.** Three bedrooms and a bathroom around a central hall, living room at the front.
7. **Cabin with a sleeping loft.** One room below, a loft above it reached by a ladder-stair.
8. **Townhouse.** Two floors: hall, living, kitchen below; two bedrooms and a bathroom above; stair in the hall.
9. **Courtyard house.** Four rooms wrapped around an open courtyard in the middle.
10. **Guest house.** Four bedrooms off a corridor, each with its own shower room.
11. **Kitchen extension.** A kitchen opening onto a terrace through glazed doors, with a counter run.
12. **Flat on a grid.** A four-room flat laid out on a track grid rather than coordinates.
13. **Pool house.** A changing room and a wc, with a pool on the deck outside.
14. **Office suite.** Reception, two offices and a meeting room off a short corridor.
15. **Long narrow house.** Five rooms in a single row, front door at one end.
16. **House with a utility.** Kitchen, utility and a back door onto the yard, plus a living room.
17. **Loft conversion.** One big room under the roof and a shower room, with the stair arriving in the corner.
18. **Shop.** A shop floor, a storeroom behind it and a staff wc, street door at the front.
19. **Holiday let.** Two bedrooms, a living room, a covered terrace and an outdoor shower.
20. **Semi-detached with a void.** Two floors, the living room double height, so the upper floor has a void over it.
